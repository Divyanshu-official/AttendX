How AttendX works

Students don't need to enter a session code. AttendX verifies the authorized classroom network automatically.

1 Teacher starts AttendX
2 Students connect to the hotspot
3 AttendX verifies the network
4 Student registers or marks attendance
🔐

Network Verification

AttendX checks whether the device is connected to the authorized classroom network before allowing protected actions.

📝

Student Registration

Students can register their details before attendance is enabled by the teacher.
